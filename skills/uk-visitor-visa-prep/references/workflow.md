# Full Case Workflow

## Source priority

Use sources in this order when facts conflict:

1. Submitted or saved online application form
2. Current official identity, immigration, education, employment, and bank records
3. Applicant or sponsor's direct clarification
4. Draft letters and itineraries
5. Third-party examples and templates, which are never factual sources

Record conflicts instead of choosing the most convenient value.

## Passes

### Pass 1: Read-only intake

- Inventory every relevant file and identify duplicates, sample files, scans, and translations.
- Inspect PDFs and images without changing them.
- Separate applicant, sponsor, relationship, residence, travel, finance, and return-reason evidence.
- List unreadable, incomplete, expired, or missing pages.

### Pass 2: Fact extraction

- Transcribe names exactly as shown in passports and forms.
- Normalize dates to `YYYY-MM-DD` in the ledger while preserving the displayed source format in notes.
- Store each monetary amount with currency, period, owner, source document, and page or transaction reference.
- Distinguish income, family support, internal transfers, refunds, savings, and one-off credits.

### Pass 3: Consistency audit

- Reconcile trip cost with applicant and sponsor contributions.
- Compare monthly support and living costs with bank activity without assuming every transaction has the same purpose.
- Check that intended travel falls within passport and legal-residence validity.
- Treat graduation, enrolment, approved leave, employment, tenancy, and close family as return reasons only when current evidence supports them.
- Flag recent large deposits, circular transfers, missing statement pages, and unexplained account ownership.

### Pass 4: Drafting

- Draft the itinerary first, then financial support statement, then cover letter.
- Keep the cover letter explanatory rather than promotional.
- Cite document names consistently and avoid attaching weak duplicates merely to enlarge the pack.

### Pass 5: Quality assurance

- Search all outputs for `[TO CONFIRM`, sample names, stale dates, and conflicting currency symbols.
- Render Word and PDF outputs and inspect each page.
- Confirm final files contain no private metadata from another applicant.

## Approval points

Obtain the user's factual confirmation before finalizing uncertain numbers, relationship claims, unusual transaction explanations, or translator details. The applicant must personally review the online form, declarations, payment, biometrics booking, upload selection, and submission.
